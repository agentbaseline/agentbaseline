# Provenance and commercial interests

**This work was convened by companies that sell products in the area it describes.** Read this
page before you weigh anything else here. It is disclosed once, plainly, in one place, rather
than being distributed into footnotes.

## What this means for how you should read it

**Three of the six outcomes — Discover, Observe and Respond — are not sold by any convening
organization.** Those sections were written without a commercial interest pulling on them, and
they are correspondingly less developed than Authorize, Constrain and Validate. That asymmetry
is a defect, not a signal about which outcomes matter. We are naming it because a reader would
otherwise have to detect it, and detecting it unaided would rightly cost us your trust.

If a section reads like a product datasheet, that is a bug. Open an issue.

## The test applied to ourselves

The six outcomes are stated in the baseline as the minimum category test for a control plane.
Applied honestly:

> **By the baseline's own test, none of the three convening organizations is a complete
> implementation.** Each covers part of the problem. Any of them telling you otherwise is
> contradicting a document they co-authored.

That sentence exists so that you can use it on us.

## What we do not claim

We do not describe this work, this group, or this property as independent. It is not. It is a
draft written by commercially interested parties, published in the open, versioned, and opened
to public comment and to outside maintainers — with the intent of donating it to a community
body as it matures. Those are the mechanisms on offer. They are checkable in this repository,
and they are the only basis on which we are asking to be taken seriously.
