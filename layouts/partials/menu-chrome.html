{{/* The shared skin for every details.menu dropdown in the utility bar. Two components wear it: the Artifacts menu, on every page, and the hamburger site menu on controls.html and 404.html. That is why the rules are not inside artifacts-menu.html — the site menu would then be styled only on pages that happen to also carry the Artifacts menu, and would lose its skin the day someone used it alone. Every including menu prints it, so the two layouts with two menus carry these rules twice. That is deliberate. Guarding the second copy behind .Store looks tidier and is a trap: hugo server reuses the page object across incremental rebuilds, the flag survives from the previous build, and the rules stop being emitted at all — a blank skin that only appears after an edit and never in a clean build. Identical rules twice cost under a kilobyte before compression and cannot fail. */}}
